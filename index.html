CLARITY & STANDARDS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Straightforward answers regarding insurance documentation, schedules, warranties, and project requirements.

We provide photographic evidence of hail strikes, wind uplift, and moisture intrusion, accompanied by itemized scope measurements. We help you document insurance claims thoroughly; however, claim approval, deductible obligations, and settlement decisions remain strictly subject to your insurer's policy terms.

Most standard residential asphalt roofs are installed in approximately 1 to 2 days, weather permitting. Complex multi-tier rooflines, metal panel fabrications, or unexpected decking rot discovered during tear-off can extend the schedule. We keep you updated daily on progress.

Class 4 impact-resistant architectural shingles and standing seam 24-gauge steel roofs offer strong resistance against hail and high wind speeds. Synthetic underlayment and ridge vent combinations also significantly reduce attic thermal buildup during Texas summers.

We provide warranty-backed workmanship (terms apply, detailed in your proposal) along with standard manufacturer material warranties. Specific coverage periods vary depending on the chosen material brand and system specifications.

Yes. Whenever required by local building departments in Dallas, Plano, Frisco, Irving, or Arlington, we file necessary municipal permits and schedule the mandatory post-installation city inspections.

We never ask for full payment upfront. In accordance with Texas consumer standards, typical structures involve an initial material deposit upon order confirmation, with remaining balances due only after material delivery, final installation, and project walk-through.
